## Activity

# 1.1. The Problem Statement 
- At a small cafe , the customer bills are calculated manually. We need to build a python program that helps calculate the bills automatically. 

# 1.2. The Inputs 
- Customer name 
- Coffee quantity
- Tea quantity
- Sandwich quantity

# 1.3. The Outputs 
- Customer Receipt 
- Total amount of price

# 1.4. Process Flow 
- Enter Customer Name 
- Enter Coffee Quantity 
- Enter Tea Quantity
- Enter Sandwich Quantity 
- Calculate Total Amout 
- Print Overall Receipt 

# 1.5. Constraints 
- Quantity must be a whole number.
- Prices are fixed:
- Coffee = RM8.50
- Tea = RM6.00
- Sandwich = RM12.00

## Decomposition of the Problem 
- Get customer information.
- Get food quantities.
- Calculate total bill.
- Print receipt.
